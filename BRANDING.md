# _FIRST_® Branding & Lockup Guidelines

MARSLib is a professional-grade framework for the **_FIRST_® Robotics Competition**. To maintain the integrity of the **_FIRST_®** brand and our partnership with the program, all contributors and agents must follow these guidelines.

## 1. Naming Conventions

The term **_FIRST_®** must always be:
- **Italicized**: `_FIRST_` or `*FIRST*`.
- **All Caps**: `FIRST`.
- **Trademarked**: Accompanied by the registered trademark symbol `®`.
- **Correct Format**: `_FIRST_®`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This rule applies to all documentation, website content, and public-facing project materials.

## 2. Logo Lockup Rules

When using official **_FIRST_®** logos:
- **No Modifications**: Never alter, animate, distort, or recolor the logos.
- **Maintain Proportions**: Always scale logos proportionally. Never stretch or skew.
- **Clear Space**: Respect the "safety zone" (minimum clear space) around the logo.
- **Full Color**: Use the full-color version whenever possible.

## 3. Team Identification

As per the official policy, our team identification (**MARS 2614**) must appear in conjunction with the **_FIRST_®** names or logos on all materials.

## 4. Reference Links

For the complete legal specifications and the latest assets, visit the official **_FIRST_®** branding portal:
- **[firstinspires.org/brand](https://www.firstinspires.org/brand)**
