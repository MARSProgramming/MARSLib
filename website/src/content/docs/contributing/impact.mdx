**Impact** is how we measure our true success. While competitive performance matters, the real endgame of **MARS 2614**—and the FIRST Robotics Competition as a whole—is the positive transformation of our culture.

## The 2017 Chairman's Legacy
As the 2017 FIRST Championship Chairman's (Impact) Award winners and Hall of Fame members, MARS operates on "The MARS Plan": **Engage, Inspire, Sustain, Progression of Programs, and Creating Leaders/Innovators.**

MARSLib is a direct extension of that plan. By formally packaging our entire software infrastructure into an accessible, heavily documented, and student-friendly framework, we are engaging teams worldwide.

## Elevating the Floor
We don't want MARSLib to just be a toolkit for the top 1% of teams. Our Impact strategy focuses on "elevating the floor":
- Releasing comprehensive **Zero-to-MARS** tutorials that walk rookie programmers through complex setups.
- Distributing **VS Code Extensions** with built-in AI "ProjectDoctors" that autonomously diagnose wiring and logic issues, acting as an automated mentor for teams that may lack experienced software coaches.
- Standardizing explicit **IO Abstraction** layers so that under-resourced teams can still write world-class logic before they even have the funding to purchase motors.

When you contribute to MARSLib, you are actively participating in a global effort to make advanced computer science education free, robust, and impactful for thousands of students.
