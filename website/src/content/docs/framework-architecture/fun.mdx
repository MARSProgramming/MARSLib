At the end of the day, building robots is supposed to be **Fun**! 

In **MARS 2614**, we celebrate technical breakthroughs—like a perfectly tuned PID loop, a smooth Shoot-On-The-Move autonomous routine, or a successful MegaTag 2.0 setup—as team-wide victories.

## The Thrill of the Swerve
There is an undeniable excitement in watching a 120-pound machine strafe across the carpet at 18 feet per second, calculating physics and tracking targets at 250 times a second. We believe that **High Performance is Fun.** 

When students work incredibly hard debugging a challenging piece of code, the euphoria of seeing the `BUILD SUCCESSFUL` terminal output followed by a flawlessly executed autonomous auto-path is electric. 

## Celebrating the Wins
- We celebrate when the simulation works perfectly *the first time*.
- We cheer when a complex geometry transformation algorithm seamlessly calculates target trajectories.
- We embrace the fun of competition by ensuring that our framework's strict standards actually *reduce* the stress on the pit crew, leaving more time to enjoy the camaraderie of the event.

Building a world-class robot takes immense effort, but with MARSLib handling the heavy structural lifting, students have more time to focus on the fun parts: tuning, innovating, driving, and celebrating!
