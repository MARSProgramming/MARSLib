In the FRC 2026: **REBUILT** game, absolute field localization is critical. MARSLib's `MARSVision` subsystem combines data from multiple cameras and strictly filters out "hallucinations" using techniques pioneered by elite teams. We utilize **[AprilTags](https://docs.wpilib.org/en/stable/docs/software/vision-processing/apriltag/apriltag-intro.html)** as the primary landmarks for global pose estimation.


  ## 1. Strict Rejection Filters

  
Vision poses are frequently wrong during high-speed gameplay. Before a measurement reached the Pose Estimator, it must survive five strict boundary checks:

  <ul >
    <li >**Z-Height Hallucinations:** If the pose estimates the robot is flying (Z > 0.5m), it's rejected.</li>
    <li >**Out of Bounds:** If the pose is tracked outside the physical field, it's rejected.</li>
    <li >**Motion Blur:** If the gyro reports > 120&deg;/s yaw rate, vision is entirely blocked.</li>
    <li >**Beaching (Pitch/Roll):** If you ride over an obstacle and tilt > 15&deg;, the pose is rejected.</li>
    <li >**Ambiguity:** Low quality single-tag solutions are discarded.</li>

  ## 2. Quadratic StdDev Scaling

  
Vision poses should mathematically never be trusted equally. A pose from 6 meters away is tiny on the camera sensorâ€”1 pixel of error dramatically shifts the calculated location.


  
MARSLib enforces **Quadratic StdDev Scaling**. Trust in vision decays *exponentially* at long range, stopping the robot from making violent odometry correction jumps based on far-away tags.


  :::note
**MegaTag / Multi-Tag Boost**


    When multiple AprilTags are visible, geometric ambiguity essentially drops to zero. Our code automatically scales down standard deviations by `x0.1`, dramatically tightening the Pose Estimator's trust.
:::

  ## 3. Simulating Imperfection

  
To ensure tuning works globally, the `AprilTagVisionIOSim` in MARSLib purposefully injects:

  <ul >
    <li >**Gaussian Noise:** StdDev matched jitter scaled by distance.</li>
    <li >**Dropped Frames:** A 5% chance every frame that no pose is returned.</li>
    <li >**Latency:** Simulates a 10ms-30ms phase delay offset.</li>
