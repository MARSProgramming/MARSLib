Discovery & Open Architecture

At MARS 2614, the core value of Discovery drives us to continually explore better ways of teaching, programming, and architecting our robotic systems. We embrace the challenge of mastering advanced control theory, zero-allocation algorithms, and sophisticated simulation environments.

Open-Source Learning

Section titled “Open-Source Learning”

The FRC community thrives when knowledge is openly shared. We treat our codebase not as a “secret weapon,” but as a public classroom. By exploring the MARSLib framework, students can discover:

  1. Zero-Allocation Practices: Learning how to manage memory safely without relying on the Java Garbage Collector, preventing 20ms delays during critical loop cycles.
  2. Deterministic Replay: Understanding how to utilize AdvantageKit to recreate past matches with bit-perfect accuracy, turning every failure into a tangible learning opportunity.
  3. Advanced Physics: Peeking under the hood of dyn4j to discover how rigid body kinematics influence autonomous robots, empowering students to build custom testing environments.

We believe that true Discovery is about pushing beyond what you already know. Rather than spoon-feeding students black-box solutions, MARSLib explicitly exposes the underlying math—from matrix regressions in our Swerve Odometry to time-of-flight interpolation via our SOTM (Shoot-On-The-Move) solver.

Never stop asking “Why does this work?”
