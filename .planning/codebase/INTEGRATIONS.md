# Integrations

## External Services & APIs
- **AdvantageScope**: Log viewing and 3D simulation analysis. Integrated via `advantagescope_layout.json`.
- **GitHub Actions**: Continuous Integration. Runs tests and spotless linting on push/PR.
- **NetworkTables 4 (NT4)**: Used for data streaming between the robot and coprocessors/dashboards.
- **WPILib Vendor Dependencies**:
  - PathPlanner (Auto routines)
  - CTRE Phoenix 6 (Motor controllers)
  - REV Robotics (Motor controllers)
  - PhotonVision (Vision processing)
